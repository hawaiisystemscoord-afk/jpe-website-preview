Cesspool Conversion to Septic Systems

Hawaiʻi's Act 184 requires property owners with cesspools to upgrade to compliant wastewater systems. JPE guides you through every step, from site evaluation to permit approval.

What JPE Provides

Evaluation, Design, and Permitting Support

Hawaiʻi's Act 184, passed in 2015, requires property owners in Hawaiʻi to convert or replace existing cesspools with compliant wastewater treatment systems. The statewide deadline is 2050, though the Department of Health may designate specific conversion areas ahead of that date.

A cesspool allows untreated sewage to seep directly into the ground. On Hawaiʻi Island, where groundwater is a primary drinking water source for many residents, this poses a serious public health and environmental risk. Conversion protects your property value, your family's health, and the island's water quality.

JPE provides a complete service pathway for property owners navigating this requirement:

  • Site evaluation: Review of TMK records, existing cesspool condition, soil characteristics, setback distances, and groundwater depth.
  • Engineered design: licensed Hawaiʻi PE designs a compliant wastewater system tailored to your property, including soil percolation analysis, system sizing, and layout plans.
  • Permitting support: JPE prepares and submits the complete permit package to the Hawaiʻi Department of Health, managing all correspondence until approval is granted.
Why Conversion Matters

Protect Your Property, Your Community, and Your Water

Environmental Protection

Cesspools allow untreated sewage to enter groundwater. Hawaiʻi Island's aquifers supply drinking water for many residents. Converting to a compliant system protects the island's most vital resource.

Property Value

Properties with non-compliant cesspools face increasing regulatory risk. A converted, permitted system protects your property value and avoids potential compliance requirements from DOH.

Regulatory Compliance

Act 184 gives DOH authority to designate specific conversion areas with accelerated deadlines. Starting the process early ensures you are compliant before any mandatory deadline applies to your property.

Early Planning Saves Time and Money

Starting the conversion process early gives JPE time to evaluate your property, identify the best system type, and navigate DOH permitting. A proactive approach also helps you plan and budget for the investment over time.

How It Works

The Cesspool Conversion Process

1

Site Evaluation

JPE reviews your property's TMK records, current cesspool condition, soil characteristics, setback distances, and groundwater depth to determine what conversion options are feasible.

2

Engineered Design

A licensed Hawaiʻi PE designs a compliant wastewater system tailored to your property. This includes soil percolation analysis, system sizing, layout plans, and all engineering calculations required by DOH.

3

Permit Application

JPE prepares and submits the complete permit package to the Hawaiʻi Department of Health, managing all correspondence and responding to reviewer comments until approval is granted.

4

Construction Oversight

While construction is performed by qualified contractors, JPE can provide oversight to ensure the system is installed according to the engineered plans and DOH requirements.

Island-Specific Expertise

Why Hawaiʻi Island Is Different

Hawaiʻi Island's geology is unlike anywhere else in the United States. Volcanic terrain, diverse soil profiles, and complex hydrology mean that a one-size-fits-all approach to wastewater design simply does not work.

Volcanic Soils

Volcanic soils, lava rock layers, and alluvial deposits vary dramatically even across short distances, directly affecting percolation rates and system type selection.

Rainfall Variation

Hawaiʻi Island ranges from arid leeward areas to extremely wet windward zones. Rainfall intensity and frequency influence drainage design and system sizing.

Groundwater Depth

Seasonal and spatial variation in the water table affects setback requirements and the type of treatment system that can be installed on any given property.
