Grading and Drainage Plans

Permit-ready grading and drainage plans prepared by a licensed Professional Engineer in Hawaiʻi. Designed for Hawaiʻi Island's unique rainfall, volcanic terrain, and county requirements.

Site Civil Engineering

Why Grading and Drainage Matter for Hawaiʻi properties

Hawaiʻi Island's steep terrain, heavy rainfall, and volcanic soils create runoff challenges. Without a proper plan in place, water can wash away driveways, undermine building foundations, and cross property lines into disputes with neighbors.

JPE designs grading and drainage systems that handle real island conditions, from the wettest windward areas to the drier leeward slopes. JPE's plans ensure water is directed away from structures, managed through the site responsibly, and routed in compliance with DOH and county standards.

What We Do

Grading and Drainage Services

Site Grading Design

Detailed cut-and-fill calculations, slope design, and earthwork plans for residential and commercial sites. JPE ensures final grade directs water away from structures and meets county standards.

Stormwater Drainage Plans

Complete drainage plans including swales, culverts, pipes, and retention features sized for Hawaiʻi Island rainfall intensity. JPE designs systems that handle the volume and velocity of island runoff.

Erosion and Sediment Control

Erosion and sediment control plans required during construction. JPE designs measures to keep soil in place and protect nearby waterways from sediment runoff.

Driveway and Access Road Design

Graded driveways and access roads that manage water effectively, maintain proper crown and cross-slope, and meet county requirements for ingress and egress.

Building Pad Preparation

Building pad plans that establish a stable, properly graded foundation platform. JPE coordinates with builders and surveyors to ensure the pad meets design specifications.

Retaining Wall Coordination

Grading plans often require retaining walls on steep terrain. JPE coordinates wall design with overall site grading to ensure structural integrity and proper water management.

Island-Specific Expertise

Hawaiʻi Island Site Conditions

Every property on Hawaiʻi Island presents unique grading and drainage challenges. JPE's plans account for the conditions specific to your site, from extreme rainfall to volcanic bedrock.

Volcanic Bedrock

Hawaiʻi Island's volcanic geology means bedrock can be close to the surface in some areas. JPE designs grading plans that work around or through rock, adjusting cut depths and drainage paths accordingly.

High Rainfall Areas

Windward areas receive some of the highest rainfall in the world. JPE can account for heavy rainfall, runoff paths, and site-specific drainage requirements during design.

Steep Slopes

Slopes for Hawaiʻi properties can be dramatic. JPE designs stable slope grades and erosion controls to keep properties safe on steep terrain.

Seasonal Streams

Many properties border seasonal streams that carry significant flow during heavy rains. JPE designs crossings and drainage features that handle peak stream flow without flooding the site.

How It Works

The Grading and Drainage Process

JPE follows a structured process from initial site evaluation through construction oversight. Each step builds on the last to produce plans that are complete, accurate, and ready for permit review.

1

Site Evaluation

JPE begins with a site visit to assess topography, existing drainage patterns, access points, and any constraints. JPE reviews available survey data and property records.

2

Drainage Analysis

JPE analyzes the existing terrain: slopes, elevations, soil conditions, and natural drainage paths: to understand how water moves across your property.

3

Plan Design

JPE prepares detailed grading and drainage plans showing proposed grades, cut-and-fill areas, drainage features, and how the final site will manage water flow.

4

DOH/County Coordination

JPE submits the complete plan package to DOH and/or the county. JPE handles agency coordination and responds to any review comments.

When You Need Us

Common Situations Requiring Grading and Drainage Plans

  • New home construction: County and DOH require grading and drainage plans before a building permit is issued.
  • Subdivision development: Lot splits and new subdivisions need engineered grading and drainage to meet regulatory standards.
  • Driveway or access road: Driveways must manage runoff, maintain proper slope, and meet county ingress/egress requirements.
  • Erosion repair: Properties experiencing erosion or drainage issues may need a professional plan to address the problem permanently.
  • Building permit application: Most construction permits for Hawaiʻi properties require grading and drainage plans prepared by a licensed PE.
