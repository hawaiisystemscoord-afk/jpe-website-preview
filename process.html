How JPE Works

Clear steps from your first call through final plans. No surprises.

From First Call to Final Plans

Every Hawaiʻi property is different. JPE approaches each project with the same commitment: clear communication at every stage, transparent pricing before work begins, and engineering built around local soil conditions, topography, and agency requirements.

JPE walks you through the process step by step so you always know what comes next. Here is how it works.

The Process

Five Steps, Clear Expectations

1

Share Your Project Details

Contact JPE with your property TMK number, address, district or area, and the type of project you need, cesspool conversion, IWS/septic design, grading plan, drainage plan, or other site civil work. JPE uses this information to understand your situation.

2

Initial Review

JPE assesses your site conditions, regulatory requirements, and design options. JPE reviews available records, evaluates topography and soil conditions, and determines the most appropriate approach for your property.

3

Design Development

Plans are prepared to meet DOH and County standards by a licensed Professional Engineer. JPE performs the necessary engineering analysis, prepares drawings and calculations specific to your property, and completes an internal review before finalizing the design.

4

Permitting Support

JPE coordinates submissions to the Hawaiʻi Department of Health, County of Hawaiʻi, or other agencies as required. JPE handles resubmissions and responds to agency feedback, keeping you informed throughout the review process.

5

Construction Support

JPE remains available for questions during installation and can conduct site visits to verify that construction complies with the approved plans. JPE documents as-built conditions and coordinates inspections with the relevant agencies.
