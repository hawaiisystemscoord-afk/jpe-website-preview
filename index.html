Site Conditions

Why Hawaiʻi Site Conditions Matter

Hawaiʻi's geology, rainfall patterns, and volcanic terrain require site-specific engineering. Soil profiles, rainfall intensity, and permeability can change quickly across short distances, affecting wastewater performance and stormwater design.

Jim Pedersen and the JPE team bring local experience to every project. Plans are built around the actual site conditions, including soil, rainfall, slope, access, and permitting requirements. This approach supports smoother review and more reliable long-term performance.
