Site Conditions

Why Hawaiʻi Site Conditions Matter

Hawaiʻi's geology, rainfall patterns, and volcanic terrain create conditions that standard mainland engineering assumptions simply don't account for. Rapidly changing soil profiles, high-intensity rainfall events, and variable permeability across short distances all affect how wastewater systems perform and how stormwater must be managed.

Designs that work elsewhere often fail here because they don't account for these island-specific conditions. Jim Pedersen and the JPE team bring local experience to every project. Plans are built around what the land actually is. This approach reduces permit delays and results in systems that perform reliably over time.
