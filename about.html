About JPE Civil & Environmental Engineers

Licensed civil and wastewater engineering for Hawaiʻi properties. Local expertise, clear communication, practical solutions.

Who Is JPE?

JPE Civil & Environmental Engineers is a civil engineering firm serving Hawaiʻi properties. Jim Pedersen, Professional Engineer License No. 12035C, leads JPE and personally reviews every set of plans JPE produces.

JPE specializes in Hawaiʻi DOH IWS-listed wastewater system design, cesspool conversion under Act 184, and site civil engineering for residential and commercial properties across Hawaiʻi. JPE is listed on the Hawaiʻi Department of Health's Individual Wastewater System (IWS) Engineer registry.

Local Expertise

What Sets JPE Apart

  • - Local knowledge of Hawaiʻi. JPE understands the specific conditions found across the islands, volcanic terrain, rapidly changing soil profiles, and high-intensity rainfall patterns that affect engineering design.
  • - Experience with Hawaiʻi conditions. Designs that work elsewhere often fail here because they don't account for the islands' unique geology and climate. JPE builds plans around what the land actually is.
  • - Practical design approach. JPE designs systems that work within real-world constraints: budget, timeline, and the physical limitations of each property. No over-engineered solutions.
