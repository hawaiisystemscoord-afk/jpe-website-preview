Why Hawaiʻi Site Conditions Matter

Hawaiʻi's volcanic terrain, diverse rainfall patterns, and rapidly changing soil profiles create conditions that standard mainland engineering assumptions simply don't account for. Every design JPE produces is built around what the land actually is.
